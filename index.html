01 — The Sanmon山門

Charred cypress, worn stone, one gate left open.

Kage begins where the road stops: a mountain gate burned black by hand, standing in its own weather. Nothing here is decoration — the soot is how a board learns to survive a hundred rainy seasons.

Climb the worn steps and the hall lifts out of the dark, its paper screens lit from inside. Above the roofline a vermilion moon holds its place, patient, half hidden.

02 — The Approach参道

Every lantern marks a step someone chose to slow down.

The path between the gates is not the fastest way up the hill — it was never meant to be. Each stone lantern is placed exactly where the climb would otherwise ask you to hurry.

By the second torii the city noise is gone, and only the sound of your own steps on wet slate is left.

Chapter 03 — Afterlight

Afterlight

The gate does not close behind you. Take the walk whenever the noise gets loud — it is always the same path, and never the same light.
